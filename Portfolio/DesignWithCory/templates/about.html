About Cory

I'm a software engineer who's spent the last 12+ years building things that have to actually work — production backends, client-facing apps, and the occasional security audit for teams who'd rather find their own weak points first.

How I got here

I started out doing full-stack contract work in my early twenties — Twitter was one of my first real clients, back in 2011 and 2012, which taught me fast that "ship it" and "ship it right" aren't always the same thing under a deadline.

Since then I've moved between full-stack development, blockchain consulting, and security auditing, usually landing wherever a team needs someone who can own a problem end-to-end rather than just their one slice of it.

What I actually do

Most of my work falls into one of three buckets: building the thing (full-stack apps, APIs, dashboards), advising on it (blockchain architecture, technical strategy), or breaking it on purpose before someone else does (security audits, penetration testing).

Recent projects have ranged from a decentralized bridge that lets Helium's HNT token move onto EVM-compatible chains, to EstiMate — a job-estimating app I built for a painting contractor who was still quoting jobs on paper — to PwnHub, a bug-bounty automation platform I run for my own security research.

How I work

I've led engineering teams, and I've been the only engineer on a project — so I know how to operate either way. I lead with the same questions on every engagement: what are we actually solving, what does "done" look like, and how do we keep scope from quietly doubling three weeks in.

Clients get plain-English updates, not jargon, and a quote that says exactly what's included and what isn't.
