About Me

Most Impactful Project

Helium has managed to give the world a decentralized, people-powered, Omni-network protocol. Yet, its most loyal supporters - the HNT miners and holders - must still rely on centralized exchanges for exchanging their earnings and buying tokens to participate. We proposed building a bridge that allowed users to convert their HNT for Wrapped HNT (wHNT). By bridging HNT onto EVM compatible blockchains (such as Solidity), we can expose HNT to the broader market, allowing users to buy, sell and earn a yield on their tokens in a decentralized way. Expanding HNT’s reach to the larger Defi market will give users a better choice on what to do with their HNT and greater control over their digital assets.
