Services and Capabilities:

Full Stack Development

You get one person who owns the whole stack, database to UI, so nothing gets lost in translation between teams. Fewer handoffs, fewer integration surprises, and a product that actually feels like one cohesive thing.

  • –
    Understanding the Business Need — what we're actually solving, and for whom.
  • –
    Architecture & Wireframing — data models and screen flows, sketched before a line of code gets written.
  • –
    Development — clean, tested code across the stack, built to be maintained by someone other than me.
  • –
    Testing & QA — checked against real-world edge cases, not just the happy path.
  • –
    Deployment & Handoff — shipped, documented, and monitored, with a walkthrough so your team isn't locked out of your own product.
Front-End Development

This is the part your users actually touch, so it has to feel right: fast, responsive, and obvious. I build interfaces people don't have to think about.

  • –
    Understanding the Business Need — who's using this, and what they're trying to get done.
  • –
    Wireframing & Prototyping — low-fidelity flows first, so we agree on the shape before polishing pixels.
  • –
    Component Development — reusable, accessible UI built to hold up as the product grows.
  • –
    Cross-Device Testing — checked on the browsers and screen sizes your customers actually use.
  • –
    Deployment & Performance Tuning — shipped fast, and kept fast; load time is a feature.
API Development

Your API is the contract everything else depends on, get it wrong and every team downstream feels it. I design them to be predictable, documented, and hard to misuse.

  • –
    Understanding the Business Need — what data needs to move, between which systems, and how often.
  • –
    Schema & Endpoint Design — contracts and naming decided before implementation starts.
  • –
    Development — versioned, documented endpoints built for the integrations you have today and the ones you'll add later.
  • –
    Load & Security Testing — stress-tested under real traffic and checked against the OWASP basics before anyone else touches it.
  • –
    Deployment & Versioning — rolled out without breaking the clients already depending on you.
Business Analytics

Data you can't act on is just noise. I build dashboards and pipelines that answer the specific questions your team is actually asking, not a wall of charts nobody opens twice.

  • –
    Understanding the Business Need — what decision this dashboard is actually meant to drive.
  • –
    Data Modeling & Wireframing — sources mapped, then a sketch of what "answered" looks like.
  • –
    Development — pipelines and visualizations built on data you can actually trust.
  • –
    Validation & Testing — numbers double-checked against the source before anyone makes a call based on them.
  • –
    Deployment & Monitoring — live, refreshing on schedule, and flagged the moment something looks off.
Security Auditing

I find the holes before someone with worse intentions does, then tell you exactly how to close them in plain English, not a 40-page PDF nobody reads.

  • –
    Scoping & Threat Modeling — defining what's in play and what an attacker would actually go after first.
  • –
    Reconnaissance & Enumeration — mapping the real attack surface, not just what's in the docs.
  • –
    Active Testing — hands-on exploitation of what's actually exploitable, safely and within scope.
  • –
    Reporting & Remediation Guidance — findings ranked by real risk, with fixes you can hand straight to a dev.
  • –
    Retest & Verification — confirming the fixes actually closed the gap, not just quieted the scanner.
